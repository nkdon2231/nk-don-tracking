Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my shipment?

Enter your N.K DON tracking reference in the tracking section and select Track Shipment.

Where do I get my tracking reference?

Your tracking reference is provided when your shipment is registered with N.K DON Logistics.

Can I contact N.K DON about my shipment?

Yes. Contact N.K DON Logistics through WhatsApp for shipment enquiries and customer support.

Are N.K DON references carrier tracking numbers?

No. N.K DON references are independent internal shipment references issued by N.K DON Logistics.

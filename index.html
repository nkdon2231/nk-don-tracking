SUPPORT Frequently asked questions What is an NKDON tracking reference? It is an internal shipment reference assigned by NKDON Tracking Logistics to identify a consignment within the NKDON system. Can I track a shipment online? Yes. Enter the NKDON tracking reference in the tracking section. The system displays only information made available through the public tracking service. Are NKDON references DHL, FedEx or UPS numbers? No. NKDON references are independent internal references and should not be represented as another carrier's tracking numbers. Why can't I see sender contact details? Private customer contact information is kept out of the public tracking result.

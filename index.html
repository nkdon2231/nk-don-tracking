Frequently Asked Questions

What is a consignment reference?

It is the unique NKDON reference assigned to a registered shipment.

What if my reference is not found?

Check the reference carefully. If it remains unavailable, contact NKDON support.

Can shipment status change?

Yes. Authorized administrators can update shipment status as the consignment progresses.

Can I contact NKDON?

Yes. Contact support through WhatsApp and include your consignment reference.

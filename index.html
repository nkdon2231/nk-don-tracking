Frequently Asked Questions

What is an NKDON tracking reference?

It is an internal shipment reference assigned by NKDON Tracking Logistics for identifying and tracking a shipment within the NKDON system.

Can I track my shipment online?

Yes. Enter your NKDON tracking reference in the tracking section of this website.

Are NKDON tracking numbers issued by DHL or FedEx?

No. NKDON references are independent internal references and should not be represented as DHL, FedEx, UPS or another carrier's tracking numbers.
